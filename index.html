The quality gap

Certification is the baseline. Execution is the risk.

PV quality risk often appears in the space between an approved design and day-to-day production: uncontrolled material changes, weak traceability, process drift, inconsistent inspection, calibration gaps, workmanship escapes, and incomplete corrective action.

Essa's Solar Expertise Services is structured around that reality. The goal is not to produce a generic audit score. It is to identify what can actually affect reliability, delivery, acceptance, and commercial exposure — then document the evidence clearly enough to support action.

Independent eyes. Evidence-first reporting. Practical closure.

Project approach

Four quality gates. One chain of evidence.

Inspection works best when findings connect across the project lifecycle. A material traceability gap during production should be visible at release. A shipment defect should be traceable back to process history. Corrective action should close the cause, not just the symptom.

See the full process →
1

Qualify

Assess supplier capability, QMS controls, factory readiness, certifications, equipment, staffing, and risk before production.

2

Control

Monitor incoming materials, process parameters, workmanship, in-process testing, traceability, and nonconformance handling.

3

Release

Apply agreed sampling and acceptance criteria before shipment, with clear pass, hold, or reject disposition supported by evidence.

4

Verify

Confirm corrective actions and receiving condition, then feed lessons back into supplier controls and future inspection intensity.
